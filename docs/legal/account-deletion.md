# Account and Data Deletion

*Effective Date: September 9, 2026*

This page explains how to have your TrikRide account and the personal data attached to
it deleted. It works without the app installed, from any browser, in line with how the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) says your information is handled.

## How to request deletion

Email the TrikRide support address shown in the app's Support tab, from the email
address your account is registered under, and say you want your account deleted. Include
the phone number on the account too if you have it, so the request can be matched to the
right record.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile: name, email, phone number, date of birth, and profile photo.
- For drivers: license number and expiry, the license photograph, tricycle number, and
  verification status.
- Saved locations and emergency contacts.
- Your account's sign-in credentials, so nobody can sign in to it again.

## What is kept, and why

Completed ride records (fare, pickup and destination, and the date) are kept after an
account is deleted, with the deleted account's name and contact details removed from
them, because Republic Act No. 10173 permits keeping records needed to investigate a
safety concern or a dispute raised about a specific ride, and a ride someone already took
is exactly that kind of record. A concern you reported through Support is kept the same
way, for the same reason.

## Timeframe

Requests are handled by a TrikRide administrator by hand and are completed within 30
days of the email being sent.
